# Research Evidence Gate

Use during `VERIFY`; copy results into the accepted review and viable brief. This is policy, not a
state source. Use `pass`, `concern`, `fail`, or `unknown` with a one-line basis; do not score.

| Dimension | Stop-level `fail` example |
|---|---|
| Evidence quantity | Increment too small to affect a decision |
| Evidence quality | Evidence too weak for the intended claim |
| Novelty | Recent equivalent review or no useful increment |
| Feasibility | Data or primary outcomes cannot support the method |
| Blockers | Critical issue without a practical resolution |

Any stop-level `fail` recommends `freeze` with `blocking_issue` and `reopen_condition`; it does not
delete or bust the idea.

When `BRIEF.md` selects a domain profile, append only the decision-critical dimensions activated
by that profile. Evaluate evidence by its relevance and fitness for the intended claim rather than
by method labels or vocabulary.

| Profile-specific dimension | Result | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| [Dimension activated by the brief.] | pass|concern|fail|unknown | [One line.] |

For `CREATE CHILD`, immature evidence, uncertain novelty, and a small pool are user-confirmable
warnings. Stale state, source mismatch, invalid transition, and missing metadata are hard blocks.
